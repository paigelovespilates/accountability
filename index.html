before you ask

a few things worth knowing

is this a training program?
no. you need to be certified already. this is for the part nobody teaches: actually starting. you’ll pick up a few simple tools, but the point is a date, a deadline, and someone expecting you to show up.
should I pick live or video?
live if you want me coaching you in the moment, with a booked slot holding you to it. video if you’d rather record when your week allows and get my feedback afterward. same lessons, same dates, same group chat either way.
what if I’ve never taught a class?
that’s who this is for. in week 1 you teach me your first three exercises, and each week builds from there. you don’t need to feel ready first.
does everyone start together, or can I start whenever?
everyone starts together and finishes on the same dates, with checkpoints locked in on day 1. no rolling start.
how much time does it take?
lessons are 5–15 minutes, four per week. each week you hand in a checkpoint, either live (75 minutes of coaching across the four weeks) or by video. plus time to practice, which is the whole point.
what if I miss a slot or a deadline?
you get one reschedule within the same week. if you miss the rescheduled slot or deadline, you lose it. your final class is the exception: you have up to two weeks after the program ends.
what if I can’t make it after I’ve paid?
life happens — if you can’t make this one, you can transfer your spot to the next one or gift it to a fellow instructor. no refunds once the program starts.
